Overview

Full-Funnel Growth for Your Online Store

E-commerce marketing requires a unique blend of creativity, data analysis, and technical know-how. At Ationic, we specialize in helping online stores — from Shopify and WooCommerce to custom-built platforms — attract, convert, and retain customers at every stage of the buyer journey. Our approach starts with a deep dive into your product catalog, target audience, and competitive landscape. We then build a tailored strategy that spans acquisition advertising, conversion optimization, post-purchase engagement, and loyalty programs.

We leverage every channel that matters for e-commerce: Google Shopping and Performance Max campaigns, Meta and Instagram shopping ads, Pinterest and TikTok product pins, email marketing flows, SMS marketing, and retargeting across the web. Our team optimizes your product listings with compelling copy, high-quality images, and strategic pricing. We also implement sophisticated tracking — from Facebook CAPI and Google enhanced conversions to server-side tagging — so every sale is accurately attributed and you can scale what works.

Beyond customer acquisition, we focus on retention and repeat revenue. Through segmented email flows, personalized product recommendations, loyalty programs, and win-back campaigns, we increase average order value and customer lifetime value. We treat your store as a living, growing business — continuously testing, refining, and expanding strategies to help you reach new revenue milestones.
