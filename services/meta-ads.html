Facebook & Instagram Ads That Convert

From creative strategy and copywriting to pixel implementation and Conversion API setup — we build full-funnel Meta campaigns that drive revenue.

Overview

Why Meta Ads for Your Business?

With over 3 billion monthly active users across Facebook, Instagram, and Messenger, Meta offers the largest and most engaged social audience in the world. But running profitable ads on Meta requires more than just boosting a post. It demands a strategic approach: the right audience targeting, compelling creative, airtight tracking, and continuous optimization based on real performance data.

At Ationic, we manage every layer of your Meta campaigns — from audience research and ad creative development to pixel installation, Conversion API (CAPI) integration, and funnel architecture. We build lookalike audiences from your best customers, set up retargeting sequences for warm traffic, and strictly manage frequency and relevance scores to keep costs low. Our weekly reports show you exactly where every rupee went and what it returned.

Creative Strategy & Copywriting

High-performing ad creatives, carousels, reels, and copy tailored to your audience and optimized for each funnel stage.

Conversion API (CAPI) Setup

Server-side tracking that bypasses browser restrictions, improves event match quality, and feeds cleaner data to Meta's algorithm.

Lookalike & Custom Audiences

Data-driven audience modeling based on your customer lists, website visitors, and engaged users for precision targeting.

Retargeting Funnels

Strategic retargeting sequences that re-engage warm traffic with relevant offers, reducing CPA and accelerating conversions.
