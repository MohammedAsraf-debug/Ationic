The Full Story

Ginger PDF is a premium AI-powered document workspace platform. It offers comprehensive PDF manipulation tools (merge, split, convert, compress, protect), AI features powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (OCR, AI summaries, AI translation, AI explanation), user dashboard, subscription billing via Razorpay, owner dashboard for analytics, and Celery/Redis task queue for background processing. Features a ginger cat mascot with an orange theme.
