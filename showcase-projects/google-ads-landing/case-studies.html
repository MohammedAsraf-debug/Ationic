Detailed breakdowns of how we delivered measurable growth for our clients across different industries and campaign types.

SaaS

CloudSync Technologies

Scaling B2B Revenue Through Precision Search & Display Campaigns

4.5x ROAS Achieved
$36k Monthly Revenue
350% Revenue Growth
62% Lower CPA

The Challenge

CloudSync Technologies, a B2B SaaS company offering cloud storage solutions, was spending $8,000/month on Google Ads with inconsistent results. Their campaigns were unstructured — keyword themes mixed together, no negative keyword strategy, and generic ad copy that failed to differentiate their value proposition. Their cost per acquisition had climbed to $480, making their customer acquisition costs unsustainable for their growth targets.

Our Strategy

We conducted a comprehensive account audit and identified three critical issues: poor keyword segmentation, low Quality Scores (average 4/10), and untapped remarketing opportunities. Our strategy included:

  • Complete account restructure with granular ad groups organized by buyer intent and product features
  • Deep keyword research uncovering 340 high-intent keywords their competitors were targeting
  • Landing page optimization to align with search intent, improving Quality Scores from 4 to 9
  • Targeted Display and YouTube remarketing campaigns for website visitors
  • Smart bidding strategy transition from manual CPC to Target CPA

Key Results

Within 90 days, CloudSync's monthly revenue from Google Ads grew from $8,000 to $36,000 — a 350% increase. Their average Quality Score rose to 8.2, reducing CPC by 45%. The remarketing campaigns alone contributed $12,000 in monthly revenue at a 6.2x ROAS.

"Ationic didn't just improve our numbers — they transformed how we think about paid search. The structured approach and transparent reporting gave us confidence to triple our ad budget within six months."

— James Thornton, VP Marketing, CloudSync
E-Commerce

ModaVault

Revamping Shopping Campaigns for a Fashion E-Commerce Brand

340% Conversion Increase
5.1x ROAS Achieved
$0.38 Avg CPC
12k+ Monthly Conversions

The Challenge

ModaVault, a fast-growing fashion e-commerce brand, was running Google Shopping campaigns with unoptimized product feeds. Their products appeared for irrelevant search queries, burning through budget with a dismal 1.2x ROAS. Their product feed lacked optimized titles, missing attributes, and low-quality images that failed Google's shopping policies.

Our Strategy

We overhauled their entire Shopping campaign structure with a data-first approach:

  • Complete product feed optimization — rewritten titles with key attributes, improved descriptions, and high-res images
  • Custom product category segmentation for granular bid management
  • Negative keyword list expansion from 50 to 1,200+ terms
  • Smart shopping campaign setup with performance max campaigns
  • Seasonal bid adjustments and promotion annotations

Key Results

Conversions surged 340% while the ROAS climbed from 1.2x to 5.1x within 60 days. The optimized product feed reduced disapproved products from 23% to under 2%. Holiday season campaigns achieved a peak ROAS of 8.7x, generating $240,000 in revenue from $27,500 in ad spend.

"The product feed optimization alone was worth the investment. Our products started appearing for the right searches, and the conversion volume we saw in the first month was more than we had in the entire previous quarter."

— Sarah Kim, Marketing Director, ModaVault
Local Business

PrimeCare Dental

Driving Qualified Local Leads at Fraction of Traditional Cost

280% More Leads
60% Lower CPA
40+ Leads/Month
$18 Cost Per Lead

The Challenge

PrimeCare Dental, a multi-location dental practice, relied on traditional advertising (radio, print, billboards) costing $15,000/month with no measurable ROI. Their previous attempts at Google Ads were poorly managed — broad match keywords, no location targeting, and generic ad copy that attracted non-qualified clicks. They were spending $3,000/month with minimal results.

Our Strategy

We built a local-first strategy focused on capturing high-intent patients actively searching for dental services:

  • Local Service Ads setup with Google's LSA program for "Google Guaranteed" trust badges
  • Location-based keyword strategy targeting 25-mile radius around each clinic
  • Call extensions and location extensions for immediate phone engagement
  • Ad schedule optimization to align with office hours and booking patterns
  • Custom landing pages for each service line (cosmetic, emergency, orthodontics)

Key Results

Qualified leads jumped from 10-15 per week to over 40 per month, with cost per lead dropping from $45 to just $18. Local Service Ads alone generated 65% of all leads at a 4.2x ROAS. The practice reduced their traditional ad spend by 40% and reallocated budget to digital, achieving a higher ROI than any previous channel.

"The Local Service Ads completely changed our patient acquisition strategy. We went from hoping people would see our billboard to having patients actively call us after seeing 'Google Guaranteed' badges. The transparency and measurability are exactly what we needed."

— Dr. James Dixon, Owner, PrimeCare Dental
