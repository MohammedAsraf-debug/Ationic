Our Treatments

Comprehensive dental care using the latest technology and techniques.

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-office whitening using Philips Zoom! WhiteSpeed. Noticeable results in just one 60-minute session. Take-home kits also available.

Single and full-mouth implant restorations using Nobel Biocare titanium implants. Natural-looking, permanent solutions for missing teeth.

1-3 sessions $3,000 - $5,000 per tooth

Traditional braces and ClearCorrect invisible aligners. Correct misalignment, overcrowding, and bite issues for a straighter, healthier smile.

1-2 sessions $800 - $1,500

2 visits $1,200 - $2,000 per tooth
