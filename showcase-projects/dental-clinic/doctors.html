Meet Your Doctors

Experienced professionals dedicated to your dental health and smile.

Dr. Sarah Mitchell

DDS, Orthodontics

Dr. Mitchell graduated with honors from Harvard School of Dental Medicine and completed her orthodontic residency at UCLA. With 15 years of experience, she specializes in complex orthodontic cases including Invisalign and traditional braces for patients of all ages.

Education: Harvard DDS, UCLA Orthodontics Experience: 15 years Specializations: Orthodontics, Invisalign, Early Intervention

Dr. James Chen

DMD, Implantology

Dr. Chen earned his DMD from the University of Pennsylvania and completed advanced training in implantology at the NYU College of Dentistry. He has placed over 2,000 dental implants and is a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists.

Education: UPenn DMD, NYU Implantology Experience: 12 years Specializations: Dental Implants, Bone Grafting, Full Mouth Reconstruction

Dr. Emily Rodriguez

DDS, Pediatric Dentistry

Dr. Rodriguez received her DDS from Columbia University and specialized in pediatric dentistry at Boston Children's Hospital. She is certified in conscious sedation and specializes in treating children with dental anxiety and special needs.

Education: Columbia DDS, Boston Children's Hospital Experience: 10 years Specializations: Pediatric Dentistry, Sedation Dentistry, Special Needs Care
