01 — Campaign Strategy

Built on Insight. Driven by Goals.

Every element of Project Aura was designed around three core objectives, targeted at a specific audience, and delivered through carefully crafted messaging pillars over 8 weeks.

Campaign Objectives

  • 🎯
    Brand Awareness

    Increase brand recognition among urban professionals aged 18–34 by 45% within target markets.

  • 📈
    Lead Generation

    Drive 1,200+ qualified leads through Facebook Lead Ads and Instagram story swipe-ups.

  • 👥
    Community Growth

    Grow Instagram following from 12K to 60K+ with authentic engagement and value-driven content.

Target Audience

18–24 Gen Z — Trend-aware, value-driven, mobile-first
25–34 Millennials — Brand-loyal, experience-seeking, career-focused
Urban Professionals — Digital natives with disposable income

Key Messaging Pillars

Inspiration

Curated lifestyle content that aligns brand with aspirational moments, daily rituals, and meaningful experiences.

Education

Actionable tips, how-to guides, and industry insights that position the brand as a trusted authority.

Community

User-generated content, behind-the-scenes access, and interactive storytelling that fosters belonging.
