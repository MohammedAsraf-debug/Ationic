Our Story

A Parisian Icon Since 1997

Nestled on the prestigious Avenue des Champs-Élysées, Le Château Noir has been a beacon of French gastronomic excellence for over two decades. Under the visionary leadership of Chef Antoine Laurent, our restaurant has earned two Michelin stars and a reputation for unforgettable dining experiences.

Every dish tells a story — of terroir, of tradition reimagined, of the finest ingredients sourced from across France. Our dining rooms blend classic elegance with contemporary comfort, creating the perfect setting for life's most memorable moments.

From our carefully curated wine cellar boasting over 800 labels to our seasonal tasting menus that celebrate the bounty of French soil and sea, Le Château Noir invites you to savor the art of fine dining at its highest expression.
