Our Chef

Chef Antoine Laurent

Chef Antoine Laurent
2 MICHELIN STARS

With over 25 years of culinary mastery, Chef Antoine Laurent has established Le Château Noir as one of Paris's most celebrated dining destinations.

Trained at Le Cordon Bleu and under the mentorship of Alain Ducasse, Chef Laurent brings a philosophy of precision, seasonality, and artistic expression to every plate. His cuisine honors French tradition while embracing modern innovation.

"Cooking is not about complexity — it is about respect for the ingredient. When you start with something extraordinary, your job as a chef is simply not to diminish it." — Chef Laurent
