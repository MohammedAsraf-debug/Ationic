Audience Strategy Deep Dive

How We Build Your Target Audiences

Every campaign starts with a thorough audience discovery process that ensures your ads reach the right people.

Data Collection

We analyze your first-party data, CRM exports, website analytics, and past campaign performance to build a complete picture of your customer base.

Audience Layering

We create a multi-layered targeting structure combining Core, Custom, Lookalike, and Retargeting audiences for full-funnel coverage.

A/B Testing

We systematically test audience segments against each other, measuring CPA, ROAS, and conversion quality to find the highest-performing combinations.

Continuous Refinement

Audiences are never static. We monitor performance daily, excluding underperforming segments and scaling winners based on real-time data.
