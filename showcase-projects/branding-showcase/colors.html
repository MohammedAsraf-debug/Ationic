Color Psychology

Near Black

Power, sophistication, authority. Establishes a foundation of premium quality and timeless elegance.

Warm Gold

Warmth, optimism, value. Creates emotional connection and signals premium positioning.

Off-White

Clarity, purity, space. Provides breathing room and focuses attention on content.

Charcoal

Depth, intimacy, stability. Adds dimension and visual weight to interfaces.
