Bloom Cosmetics began in a sun-drenched studio surrounded by wildflowers. Founder Lina Marchetti believed that what we put on our skin should be as pure as what grows in nature. In 2018, she began blending rosehip, calendula, and wild pansy into the first Bloom lip tint.

Today, every product is still made in small batches — always vegan, cruelty-free, and bottled with intention. We partner with women-led farms and use only compostable or refillable packaging. Because beauty shouldn't cost the earth.
